Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Camelody Personal Tools are used by one person only: the owner of the Google account they connect to.

  • What is accessed: only the Google data the owner grants at sign-in (for example tasks or calendar events).
  • Where it goes: it is read through Google's APIs and kept on the owner's own computer. Sign-in tokens are stored locally and never uploaded.
  • Sharing: nothing is shared, sold, or sent to any third party.
  • Revoking: access can be removed at any time from the Google Account's third-party connections page.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
